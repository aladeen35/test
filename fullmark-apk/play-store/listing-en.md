Full Mark: can you score the perfect 100? 🏆

Full Mark (فول مارك) is a family quiz game in the style of a TV game show, with a friendly cartoon host who cheers you on from the first question to the final celebration. Gather your family or friends and test your knowledge with more than 1,350 Arabic questions. No question repeats until you have played through the whole bank.

Completely free • No ads • No in-app purchases • No accounts or sign-up
Questions and interface are in Arabic.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOW A ROUND WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Every round is worth 100 points across 3 levels plus a speed bonus:

• Easy: 5 multiple-choice questions, 3 points each, only 10 seconds per question.
• Medium: 5 questions, 5 points each, 30 seconds, plus the "Plane" (الطيارة) lifeline to skip a question.
• Speed bonus: 10 rapid-fire questions in 60 seconds, 1 point each.
• Hard: 5 questions, 10 points each, 30 seconds, with lifelines: 50:50, call a friend, change the question, and the AI assistant.

Answer correctly and quickly to earn extra "speed points" on top of your round score.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WAYS TO PLAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• One device: host and player on the same phone.
• Vs. computer: beginner, experienced or expert opponent, or adaptive difficulty that adjusts to you.
• Online, two devices: create a room and share its 5-digit code.
• Online family room: several players answer at the same time on their own phones while the host runs the game.
• Bluetooth: play between two nearby phones without internet.
• Family pass-and-play challenge: 2 to 6 players take turns, with a winners' podium at the end.
• Standalone speed challenge: for fun or practice, any time.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
AI ASSISTANT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
On the hard level, type or speak your question to the AI assistant to get a hint. If a reply is ever inappropriate, report it with the "Report reply" button.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
GAME-SHOW ATMOSPHERE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• A friendly cartoon host who reacts to your answers.
• Celebrations with confetti, vibration and applause.
• Real sound effects.
• A warm theme inspired by the logo, plus an optional neon theme.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
YOUR SCOREBOARD

• A permanent leaderboard.
• Score curves over time, with dates.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
