## Full description (max 4000)
🩸 Your support saves lives.

In a single moment, a family's world can change: a patient waiting for surgery, a mother in the delivery room, a child who needs a transfusion now. Fazat Dam ("blood rescue" in Arabic) turns that moment from scattered group-chat forwards into an organised network that brings the right request to the right donor in their city: fast, safely, and without exposing anyone's phone number.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✦ Two separate networks
• Sudan and Saudi Arabia run as fully separate networks. Donors are matched only with requests in their own country.
• Pick your network on the home screen and see verified urgent requests near you.

✦ Request blood in a minute
• Blood group, units, hospital and urgency, all in one clear form.
• Every request is reviewed before donors are notified, which keeps fake requests out.

✦ Track every step
• A live timeline: verified → finding donors → donors responding → donation arranged → confirmed.
• See who responded, and look for more donors with one tap.

✦ Your number stays private
• Donors appear only as aliases such as D-7K3QX. Full names, numbers and addresses are never shown.
• Contact details are revealed only when both sides agree on a specific request, and access closes when the request ends.
• Every reveal is logged for review.

✦ You decide when to help
• Get notified when someone in your city needs your blood group.
• Accept or decline in one tap, and pause your availability at any time.

✦ Humanitarian initiatives & partners
• Blood drives, hospital support, awareness and volunteering, published by approved organisers.

✦ Arabic-first, English too
• A full right-to-left Arabic interface, with English one tap away.
• Lightweight and fast, even on weak connections.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⚕️ Important
Fazat Dam is a community coordination platform, not a medical provider. Matching is preliminary (city, declared blood group, availability). Donor eligibility and blood compatibility are decided only by the blood bank or hospital. A donation counts as confirmed only after the facility confirms it.
